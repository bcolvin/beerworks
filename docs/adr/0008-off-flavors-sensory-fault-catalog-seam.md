# 0008. Off-Flavors Catalog Seam, Sensory Detection Model, and One-Way Style Cross-Referencing

We decided to model **Off-Flavors** (`OffFlavor`) on top of the unified generic catalog architecture established in ADR 0006 (`Ingredient`, `BaseIngredientRepository`, `IngredientListViewModel`, `IngredientDetailViewModel`, `IngredientCatalogScaffold`, `IngredientCardScaffold`, `IngredientDetailScaffold`, and `FavoriteIngredientDao`) rather than introducing a parallel repository and UI stack, while tailoring three domain-specific seams:

1. **Semantic Mapping onto the Catalog Seam**: Instead of a botanical species and geographic country flag, `OffFlavor` supplies a sensory cue emoji via `countryFlag`, maps its chemical compound and perception threshold onto the italicized card sub-header, maps `substituteIds` to *"Commonly Confused With"* lookalike off-flavors, and maps `commonStyles` (`OffFlavorStyleCrossRef`) to *"Styles Where Permissible"*.
2. **One-Way Style Cross-Referencing**: While `OffFlavorDetailScreen` links out to `BeerStyle` cards where low-to-moderate levels of an off-flavor are style-appropriate (e.g., Diacetyl in Czech Premium Pale Lager, 4-VG in Weissbier), `StyleDetailScreen` intentionally omits an Off-Flavors section so style guidelines remain focused on positive formulation rather than fault lists.
3. **Sensory Detection & Thresholds in Place of a Radar Polygon**: Because a single chemical off-flavor spikes on one narrow sensory dimension rather than spanning an 8-axis flavor profile, `OffFlavorDetailScreen` replaces `SensoryRadarChart` with a structured **Sensory Detection & Thresholds Card** (breaking down `Aroma`, `Flavor`, and `Mouthfeel` modalities alongside `Perception Threshold`, `Typical Beer Range`, `Chemical Compound`, and `Meilgaard Flavor Wheel` code) plus scannable bullet lists for *Commonly Confused With*, *Typical Origins (Why It Happens)*, and *Prevention & Remedy (How to Fix It)*.
